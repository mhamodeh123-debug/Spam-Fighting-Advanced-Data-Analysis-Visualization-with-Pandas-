PART#2: Data Analysis & Visualization with Pandas 
dataset overview : 
the dataset we used in this part is AB_NYC_2019.csv it contain information about Airbnb listing in new York city in 2019 , the dataset has 48000 rows and 16 columns . the dataset has information about new York city and the people who live in the city and other data such as : name, host_id, host_name, neighbourhood, latitude, room_type, price, minimum_nights, number_of_reviews, last_review, reviews_per_month. The dataset show that  there are 221 neighbourhoods, spread over the five main boroughs in the city of new York The neighbourhoods mentioned most in this dataset are: Williamsburg , Bedford-Stuyvesant, Harlem, Bushwick, Upper West Side.The five areas mentioned above make up a substantial part of all Airbnb listings implying that the two areas Brooklyn and Manhattan are the leading areas that dominate the market regarding the provision of short term accommodation listings offered by Airbnb .
